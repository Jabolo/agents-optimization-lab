# Transfer — Agenda export

The conference team asks you to assess a proposed agenda-export ordering. Read `docs/ticket.md` and `docs/contract.md`. Use your coding agent fully to investigate, run checks and draft explanations.

Choose one workflow habit to watch—for example, your instructions, context, model, skill or delegation—and note what you expect it to help with. Letting your agent decide is a valid choice too; if you have no usual habit, choose a move for this attempt. Choose a condition and check, and predict what result would support acceptance. Inspect the actual output. In the browser notebook or this case's `RESULT.md` (one record is enough), identify the result, its limit and your decision: accept, reject, seek another observation, change approach or stop. If work is unfinished or produces no usable output, record the partial result or “inconclusive / no evidence” and your next move. An observed comparison supports a decision for the condition checked; note its limits. Stop when another observation is not worth its time, and resume with an unchecked condition.

## Examine your workflow and decide what next

Choose a move for your current uncertainty:

- **Inspect evidence:** What was actually checked, and what could still change your decision? Try [prompt card 2](PROMPT-CARDS.md) if useful.
- **Try a workflow move:** If there is a gap, ask for one targeted observation or try another available approach. Predict what it could add, then inspect the actual result and extra effort. [Prompt card 4](PROMPT-CARDS.md) offers a way to choose the next move.
- **Keep or stop:** If the result is sufficient for your scope, explain why another step is not worth it. An inconclusive result and next move are valid too.

Look back at your chosen habit: what did it contribute, repeat or leave uncertain? Consider effort, time or cost where observable; unknown is fine. Repetition can still be useful, and you may not be able to tell what a single step contributed. Compare what happened with your expectation. A second attempt is optional; do not claim you compared approaches you did not run. To compare fairly later, use [extension 5](EXTENSIONS.md) with fresh starts and the same checks.

A patch is optional. If you edit, distinguish the original proposal from the changed version and state which one your decision assesses. This case has no `NEXT.md`. Optional `support/READ-ME-FIRST.md` describes a bounded scenario and prior observation after your own decision or inconclusive result. Record elapsed time and reliable usage/cost only when available. Reflect on what you will keep, change or stop in your own work, under what condition and why.
