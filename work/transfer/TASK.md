# Transfer — Agenda export

The conference team asks you to assess a proposed agenda-export ordering. Read `docs/ticket.md` and `docs/contract.md`. Use your coding agent fully to investigate, run checks and draft explanations.

Choose a condition and check, and predict what result would support acceptance. Inspect the actual output. In the browser notebook or this case's `RESULT.md` (one record is enough), identify the result, its limit and your decision: accept, reject, seek another observation, change approach or stop. If work is unfinished or produces no usable output, record the partial result or “inconclusive / no evidence” and your next move. An observed comparison supports a decision for the condition checked; note its limits. Stop when another observation is not worth its time, and resume with an unchecked condition.

A patch is optional. If you edit, distinguish the original proposal from the changed version and state which one your decision assesses. This case has no `NEXT.md`. Optional `support/READ-ME-FIRST.md` describes a bounded scenario and prior observation after your own decision or inconclusive result. Record elapsed time and reliable usage/cost only when available. Reflect on what you will keep, change or stop in your own work, under what condition and why.
