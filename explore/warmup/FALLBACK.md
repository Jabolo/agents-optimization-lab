# Working with limited tools

You can use any available agent, local tools, or the visible task, trace and contract. Choose a condition and predict what a check could establish. Record your own current decision or “inconclusive / no evidence,” the actual evidence and your next move. Partial work can be saved and resumed later.

Where `support/READ-ME-FIRST.md` exists, it explains optional case-specific support. Read it after your own decision or inconclusive result. For any review case, also assess the `NEXT.md` report first. Its scenario and saved observation cover a bounded earlier run of the original case, not your own patch or machine. Predict the scenario outcome before inspecting or running it; compare actual output with the contract and record what it establishes and leaves open. Saved output is an evidence replay, with no time or cost measurement for your work.

If Python or the agent is unavailable and this case has no support folder, use its visible trace and contract to state what you can and cannot conclude. No runtime result should be claimed without one. See the bundle's `HELP.md` for recovery and a fresh copy; do not delete your work.
